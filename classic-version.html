A Birthday Party They Actually Get To Star In

Unlike soft play centres, trampoline parks, or mobile disco hire, CoverStar Kids Parties give children an immersive creative experience inside Prohibition Recording Studios. Supported by our sound engineers, your child and their friends step into a real soundproof vocal booth, wear professional headphones, and record their favourite songs together.

The 3-Stage Pop Star Experience

01

Rehearse

Warm up the vocal cords with your sound engineer, check lyrics on studio projector screens, and rehearse group vocals in our 1920s lounge space.

02

Record

Step inside our soundproof vocal booth! Record group vocals and optional solo takes through high-end studio microphones with full backing tracks.

03

Celebrate

Kick back on underlit studio benches, enjoy birthday cake, strike a pose for paparazzi photos, and take home custom personalised vinyl-style CDs!

Will My Child Enjoy It?

Yes, absolutely! Parents frequently ask whether their child needs to be a confident or talented singer. The answer is no! Our experiences focus on laughter, teamwork, and shared entertainment rather than vocal perfection.

Shy children thrive because recordings are completed in small friendly groups of 4 to 6 before any solo takes are offered. There is zero pressure, and our party hosts keep everyone engaged with pop trivia, makeup, and prop photos.

Parent Essentials Checklist

  • ✔️ Duration: 1.5 to 2 hours of hosted studio time.
  • ✔️ Supervision: At least one parent or guardian must remain at the studio.
  • ✔️ Food & Cake: Kitchen facilities available. Bring your own cake or order hot pizza!
  • ✔️ Keepsakes: Every child leaves with custom physical or digital music recordings.
