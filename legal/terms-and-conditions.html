Terms & Conditions

Party booking terms and studio conditions.

1. Booking & Deposits

Submitting an availability enquiry form does not reserve a party date. A date is secured only after studio availability is confirmed and a £50 deposit is paid.

2. Adult Attendance Policy

At least one parent or responsible adult guardian must remain present at Prohibition Recording Studios for the entire duration of the party.

3. Guest Capacities & Additions

Gold and Platinum packages cover up to 8 guests. Ultimate covers up to 10 guests. Extra guests are charged at the specified per-person rate.
