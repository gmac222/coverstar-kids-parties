Privacy Policy

How CoverStar Kids Parties and CoverStar Experiences protect your personal information.

1. Who We Are

CoverStar Kids Parties is a specialist website operated by CoverStar Experiences (legal entity), Prohibition Recording Studios, 24 Arrad Street, Liverpool City Centre, L1 3BP. Telephone: 0800 689 7827.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect parent and carer contact details submitted via our availability enquiry form (parent name, email, UK telephone number, preferred party date, child turning age, and optional notes). We do not collect full personal data directly from children.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly to check party date availability, communicate booking arrangements, and provide studio customer service. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Child Safeguarding & Photo Consent

Photographs and videos are taken during parties only with explicit parent or guardian consent. Identifiable images of children are never published without prior parental permission.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding data protection or your rights under UK GDPR, please email us at info@coverstarexperiences.co.uk.
