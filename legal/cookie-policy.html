Cookie Policy

Information about cookies used on coverstarkidsparties.co.uk

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help the site function smoothly and provide anonymous analytical data.

2. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for basic site navigation, form security, and responsive features. These cannot be disabled.

3. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure website traffic, page visits, and form enquiry submissions anonymously. No personally identifiable information is stored in analytics cookies.
