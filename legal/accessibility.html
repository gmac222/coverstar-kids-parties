Accessibility Statement

Commitment to web accessibility and studio venue access.

1. Web Accessibility Commitment

CoverStar Kids Parties is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We strive to adhere to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, incorporating high-contrast text, keyboard focus states, semantic HTML markup, and scalable font sizes.

2. Venue Accessibility

Prohibition Recording Studios is located at 24 Arrad Street in Liverpool City Centre. Please contact our team prior to booking if any guest has specific mobility or access requirements so we can make suitable arrangements.
