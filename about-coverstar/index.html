About CoverStar Experiences

Liverpool's premier recording studio party experience provider, established in 2013.

10+ Years of Unforgettable Birthday Experiences

Established in 2013, CoverStar Experiences was created to give people of all ages the opportunity to step into a professional recording studio and record music with expert sound engineers.

CoverStar Kids Parties is our dedicated specialist child brand. We operate inside Prohibition Recording Studios in Liverpool City Centre, offering structured, stress-free popstar birthday celebrations for children aged 7 to 16.

10-Time TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice Winner

Thanks to hundreds of 5-star reviews from parents, CoverStar Experiences has been awarded TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice recognition for 10 separate years (2014, 2015, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025).
