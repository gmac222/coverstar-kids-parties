# CoverStar Kids Parties Liverpool

## Website Strategy, Build Brief and AI Website Builder Prompt

Prepared 3 September 2026

## 1. Executive direction

Build this as the dedicated Liverpool kids-party website for CoverStar Experiences, not as a generic party directory and not as a disguised independent business.

The strongest commercial proposition is:

> A private pop-star birthday party in a real Liverpool recording studio, where children sing, record a song and leave with a lasting keepsake.

The website should target parents and carers searching for a kids party, children's birthday party or party venue in Liverpool, then quickly explain why CoverStar is different from soft play, trampoline parks, hall hire and mobile entertainers.

Primary conversion: availability/quote enquiry.

Secondary conversion: telephone call to 0800 689 7827.

Recommended public brand presentation:

**CoverStar Kids Parties**  
**By CoverStar Experiences**

Confirmed domain: `coverstarkidsparties.co.uk`.

## 2. Critical decisions before launch

1. **Confirm current prices.** The current dedicated kids page shows Gold £199 for up to 8, Platinum £280 for up to 8 and Ultimate £380 for up to 10. An older general packages page shows conflicting per-person prices. The new website must use one approved price source only.
2. **Confirm the precise venue address.** Current sources use both 24 and 24A Arrad Street. Use the same approved address everywhere, including the footer, contact page, schema and Google Business Profile.
3. **Confirm the age range and capacity.** The current kids page says age 7+, while other copy refers to hosts for ages 6–16 and also says “children of all ages”. Publish a single accurate rule.
4. **Confirm accessibility, parking and adult-supervision details.** Do not let the website builder invent these.
5. **Confirm safeguarding language.** State the adult attendance rule and any relevant staff/safeguarding information only where it is factually supported.
6. **Choose the SEO relationship with the parent site.** Do not publish substantially duplicated sales pages on both domains.

## 3. Recommended parent/child website architecture

## 5. Positioning and messages

### Core promise

The birthday child and friends become pop stars for the day in a working professional studio, supported by an experienced sound engineer. They rehearse, sing, record and celebrate together, then receive a professionally produced memory of the experience.

### Reasons to choose CoverStar

- A real recording studio, rather than a themed room or mobile karaoke setup.
- A structured, hosted experience that reduces the organising burden for parents.
- Group participation, with no need to be an experienced or confident singer.
- Professional equipment, sound engineer, on-screen lyrics and backing tracks.
- Tangible and digital keepsakes, depending on package.
- Packages lasting 1.5 to 2 hours.
- Liverpool city-centre/Georgian Quarter location.
- Operating since 2013.
- Strong independent social proof, including 422 Tripadvisor reviews and repeated Travellers' Choice recognition, subject to live verification and permitted use of badges/logos.

### Emotional angle

Write to the parent, not to the child. The tone can be energetic and celebratory, but the selling points should be reassurance, ease, inclusion, a special birthday and memories that last.

Avoid childish copy, excessive exclamation marks, generic phrases such as “magical memories” without proof, or claims that every child will sound professional.

## 8. Enquiry journey

The site should be enquiry-led unless the business already has a reliable real-time booking and payment system.

Use one short availability form with:

- Parent/carer's name
- Email
- UK telephone number
- Preferred date
- Alternative date (optional)
- Preferred time (optional)
- Child's age
- Estimated number of children
- Package of interest / “not sure yet”
- Food, accessibility or other requirements
- Consent checkbox linked to privacy policy

Confirmation message:

> Thanks. Your preferred date is not reserved yet. The CoverStar team will check availability and contact you. A £50 deposit is currently used to secure an agreed date and time, subject to confirmation of the current booking terms.

Do not say “Book now” if clicking only opens an enquiry form. Use “Check availability” or “Request a party date”.

Add click-to-call on mobile. Do not use a child-facing chatbot or collect personal data directly from children.

## 9. Visual and UX direction

The design should feel like a premium pop concert crossed with a real recording studio. It should be exciting enough for children but trustworthy and easy for parents.

### Visual characteristics

- Dark charcoal/navy base with energetic violet, electric pink or gold highlights, adjusted to the approved CoverStar identity.
- White or very pale content sections for long-form readability.
- Stage-light gradients, subtle sound-wave motifs and rounded cards.
- Strong photography with children actively singing, groups having fun, the studio equipment and physical keepsakes.
- Modern bold display heading paired with a highly readable sans-serif body font.
- Clear 16px minimum body text, generous spacing and large buttons.
- Avoid cartoon mascots, confetti overload, neon text on every section, auto-playing audio and visual clutter.

### Mobile requirements

- Build mobile first.
- Sticky bottom CTA with “Call” and “Check Date”.
- Package facts visible without opening multiple accordions.
- Tap targets at least 44px.
- Compressed responsive images and no layout shift.
- No intrusive full-screen pop-up on arrival.

## 10. Content rules

- UK English, including “personalised”, “centre” and “organising”.
- Address parents directly with “you” and use “we” for CoverStar.
- Use “children” and “kids” naturally rather than forcing one wording.
- One H1 per page; use descriptive H2 and H3 headings in logical order.
- Give the direct answer immediately below question headings.
- Keep paragraphs short and use lists/tables where they improve decision-making.
- Use inclusive wording such as birthday child rather than assuming girl/boy.
- Explain that singing ability is not required.
- Do not use unsupported superlatives such as “Liverpool's number one”.
- Do not invent prices, availability, accreditations, capacities, accessibility, safeguarding checks, parking arrangements or staff biographies.
- Never reproduce reviews inaccurately. Retain the source and link where practical.
- Avoid mentioning named current pop artists in evergreen copy unless rights and brand implications have been considered.

## 11. Local SEO and technical specification

### Homepage metadata

Title: **Kids Parties Liverpool | Pop Star Parties | CoverStar**

Meta description: **Plan an unforgettable kids' party in a real Liverpool recording studio. Compare CoverStar pop-star party packages and check your preferred date.**

### Technical essentials

- Fast, crawlable server-rendered HTML.
- Semantic HTML5 and logical heading order.
- Self-referencing canonical on every indexable page.
- XML sitemap, robots.txt and clean lowercase URLs.
- Unique title and meta description on every page.
- Open Graph and social-sharing metadata.
- Descriptive image filenames, accurate alt text and WebP/AVIF delivery.
- Breadcrumbs on internal pages.
- Proper 301 redirects if URLs change.
- GA4, Google Search Console and consent management configured correctly.
- Track form submissions, telephone taps, package CTA clicks and parent-site referrals as conversions.
- Core Web Vitals targets: LCP under 2.5 seconds, INP under 200ms and CLS under 0.1 at the 75th percentile.

### Structured data

Use truthful JSON-LD only:

- `LocalBusiness` or the closest valid subtype on the homepage/contact page.
- `Service` for the pop-star party service.
- `Offer` entries for confirmed packages and prices.
- `BreadcrumbList` on internal pages.
- `FAQPage` only for visible FAQ content and only if it complies with current Google eligibility rules.
- Do not mark up self-authored testimonials as aggregate third-party ratings.

Key entity connections should identify CoverStar Kids Parties as a service/brand operated by CoverStar Experiences at the same Liverpool studio. Link `sameAs` only to verified official profiles.

### Local relevance

Use the actual Liverpool address and practical arrival information. Mention the Georgian Quarter and proximity to Liverpool city centre only where accurate. A map, landmark-based directions, nearby parking/public-transport information and arrival instructions will be more useful than generic paragraphs about Liverpool.
