Why We Write These Guides

CoverStar Experiences has hosted children's recording studio parties in Liverpool since 2013. Over that time we have answered the same questions from thousands of parents: how much should a party cost, what works for a ten year old, how do we get a group of children to sing, and where on earth do you park in the city centre. These articles are our answers, written down. Where we recommend our own parties we say so plainly, and where another kind of party would suit your child better we say that too.
